Help

Will the calendar update automatically?

Yes. Once subscribed, future changes will appear automatically when the calendar refreshes.

What should I choose for a player with positional training?

Choose the positional calendar only. It already contains the standard age-group training sessions.

What happens when I tap a button?

Your phone or calendar app should open and ask you to confirm the subscription.

How do I remove a calendar later?

Open your phone's calendar settings, find the subscribed Magic Netball calendar and remove or unsubscribe from it.
